My Approach

The ADDIE Framework

Every project follows a proven, research-backed methodology that ensures learning solutions are aligned, effective, and measurable.

01

Analyze

Needs analysis & gap assessment. Stakeholder interviews. Define learning objectives & success metrics.

02

Design

Instructional strategy & course outline. Assessment design. Storyboards & prototypes.

03

Develop

Interactive eLearning modules. Multimedia assets. Assessments & SCORM compliance.

04

Implement

LMS deployment. Facilitator guides & learner support. Administrator training.

05

Evaluate

Learner feedback. Knowledge gain & behavior change. ROI measurement & iteration.
