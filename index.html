My Approach

THE ADDIE FRAMEWORK

A proven, systematic approach to instructional design that ensures every learning experience is purposeful, engaging, and results-driven.

01

ANALYZE

Conduct needs analysis & gap assessment. Interview stakeholders & SMEs. Define learning objectives & success metrics.

02

DESIGN

Create instructional strategy & course outline. Develop assessment strategy. Design storyboards & prototypes.

03

DEVELOP

Build interactive eLearning modules. Create multimedia assets. Develop assessments & ensure SCORM compliance.

04

IMPLEMENT

Deploy to LMS. Provide facilitator guides & learner support. Train administrators & monitor rollout.

05

EVALUATE

Collect learner feedback. Measure knowledge gain & behavior change. Calculate ROI & iterate to improve.
